# NestRunner — Steal an Egg Auto Farm for Roblox

NestRunner automates the complete egg-farming cycle in Roblox’s Steal An Egg, from selecting valuable eggs to bringing them back, hatching them, equipping pets, and selling weaker returns. The automation prioritizes eggs by potential income, so each collection run contributes to stronger long-term earnings. It travels between available areas, evaluates targets, and brings collected eggs to the player’s plot. Once an egg reaches the base, NestRunner handles placement and hatching without requiring each stage to be managed separately. Newly hatched pets can be equipped automatically, keeping the active lineup aligned with progression. When the pen fills, NestRunner sells lower-value pets while preserving the selected value threshold for stronger earners. Treadmill training and affordable speed upgrades support access to higher-value areas. Offline earnings are collected when a session begins, adding accumulated rewards to the active run. A compact control panel provides pause, resume, movement-mode, and stop controls. NestRunner brings stealing, hatching, pet management, selling, training, and upgrades into one continuous Steal An Egg auto-farm routine.

## What is this

NestRunner is an automation companion for the Roblox experience Steal An Egg. It manages the progression loop that turns stolen eggs into a productive pet collection. The routine evaluates available eggs, chooses a worthwhile target, returns it to the player’s plot, and continues through placement, incubation, equipping, and inventory upkeep. It coordinates the major farming stages as one workflow.

Target selection is based on earning potential. NestRunner compares available eggs and favors stronger opportunities within reachable areas. It can collect several eggs before returning to base tasks, keeping travel and plot management connected. When speed limits access to another area, the routine shifts to treadmill training and available upgrades.

The base cycle continues after each return. Eggs are placed, completed hatches are processed, new pets are equipped, and lower-performing pets are sold when space becomes limited. A configurable value floor keeps stronger earners out of routine sales, allowing the collection to grow without discarding pets that meet the selected income standard.

## Key Features

- **Automatic egg stealing:** Selects valuable targets using projected income and the areas currently within reach.
- **Automatic return and placement:** Carries collected eggs back to the player’s plot and places them into the farming cycle.
- **Automatic hatching and pet equipment:** Processes completed eggs and equips newly hatched pets to keep income generation moving.
- **Value-aware pet selling:** Clears lower-performing pets as pen capacity tightens while retaining pets above the chosen earning threshold.
- **Treadmill training and upgrades:** Builds movement speed and purchases available improvements to support access to later areas.
- **Offline earnings collection:** Claims accumulated earnings when the player joins and resumes the farming routine.
- **Session controls:** A movable on-screen panel keeps pause, resume, movement selection, and stop actions within reach.
- **Progression-cycle handling:** Responds to zone resets and server transitions so the routine can continue across recurring sessions.

## Why it’s convenient

NestRunner connects the main progression decisions. Egg collection follows income potential, base work continues after each trip, and training becomes part of the routine when speed determines the next available target. Players spend less time switching between stealing, hatching, selling, and upgrading.

Its value threshold gives pet management a clear priority: weaker earners make room, while pets that meet the selected standard remain in the collection. Batch collection keeps each trip purposeful, and the status panel makes it straightforward to pause or end the active routine.
